Hey, Lucija and Balazs here.

We’re the founders of WanderVienna.com – I’m Balázs, a Hungarian-born traveler living in Vienna, and Lucija is Slovenian-born. Our goal is to make planning your Vienna to Hallstatt day trip simple, easy, and stress-free, ensuring that your journey is unforgettable and hassle-free.

We do this by being tourists ourselves—we explore, try out activities, visit destinations, and then create incredibly detailed guides about them. This way, we can offer you real, tested recommendations, unlike the vague and unhelpful advice you often find on Google. Isn’t this how travel recommendations should work?

There’s no second chance when it comes to making the most of your holiday. A bad tour, a disappointing experience, or an unexpected hiccup can ruin your entire trip. This isn't Amazon—there are no refunds. That’s why we act as your boots on the ground, making sure you have a fantastic time in Austria, Slovenia, or Hungary—whether you're heading on a Hallstatt day tour from Vienna, a scenic journey to Lake Bled, or a cultural deep dive in Budapest.

We believe that travel is more than just seeing new places—it’s about spreading the goodwill and joy that we’ve been lucky enough to experience on our own journeys. By sharing what we’ve learned, we hope to create positive ripples in the travel community and beyond.

Thank you for considering WanderVienna.com as your travel planning companion. Whether you're dreaming of the breathtaking landscapes of Hallstatt, charming small towns, or other unforgettable experiences, we’re here to help make your Vienna to Hallstatt day tour seamless, memorable, and truly special.
